## Scope

General principles for describing, designing, operating, verifying, and understanding composed systems and their behavior.

## Content Development

This area is a **navigation discipline**: the eleven approved headings organize technical coverage without becoming a new universal ontology. The subject references own domain-agnostic systems concepts and link to general mathematical, modeling, information, human and physical foundations. Specialized products and security applications belong to their contextual knowledge areas.

**Wave B Batch 03** adds four substantive Drafts to the previously Reviewed Systems and Subsystems reference. Their source links and examples are available, but a separate technical audit is required before any article can be marked Reviewed.
