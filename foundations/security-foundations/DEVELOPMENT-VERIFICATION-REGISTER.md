## Governance and relationship gates

The area structure implements the explicitly requested Fundamentals-style navigation, but the working **12 area names and 96 subject memberships remain proposed and flexible**. A manuscript can be internally Reviewed while the proposed taxonomy is unratified. A source's existence does not prove every claim; a successful link check does not prove a technical model.

An adopted split/merge/relocation requires a recorded decision, old-to-new ID/path map, article/index changes, and fresh technical review as needed. This register, each area README, the [candidate inventory](SUBJECT-INVENTORY-PROPOSAL.md), and the [ownership register](CANONICAL-OWNERSHIP-REGISTER.md) must be reconciled together.
