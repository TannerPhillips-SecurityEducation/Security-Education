## Scope and canonical ownership

This working area groups **protected interests, assets & harm** concepts that share a security-specific explanatory purpose. Boundaries follow the [working inventory](../SUBJECT-INVENTORY-PROPOSAL.md), [ownership decisions](../OWNERSHIP-BOUNDARIES.md), and [governing architecture proposal](../ARCHITECTURE.md), not a rigid formal ontology. Generic science/technical concepts belong in [Fundamentals](../../fundamentals/README.md); security practices belong in [Operational Foundations](../../operational-foundations/README.md); product-specific implementations belong to the seven specialized domains.

The area may be renamed, split, consolidated, or relocated when stronger conceptual and source evidence justifies it. **A directory is a navigation home, not automatically a ratified subject taxonomy.**

## Development and relationships

New substantive references belong **inside this area directory**, directly under its `README.md`, following the [Fundamentals area pattern](../../fundamentals/mathematics-logic/README.md). Each article owns a coherent security-specific concept and links meaningfully to related areas and sources.
