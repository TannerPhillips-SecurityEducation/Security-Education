# Industrial Knowledge

Industrial Knowledge is organized primarily by **Industrial System Function**.

## Approved Navigation Categories

- [Physical Processes & Equipment](physical-processes-equipment/README.md): Production processes, machinery, motors, pumps, and industrial equipment
- [Instrumentation & Actuation](instrumentation-actuation/README.md): Sensors, instruments, transmitters, valves, and actuators
- [Control & Automation](control-automation/README.md): PLCs, RTUs, DCS controllers, automation, and control logic
- [Supervisory Systems](supervisory-systems/README.md): SCADA, HMIs, supervisory monitoring, and operator interfaces
- [Safety & Protection](safety-protection/README.md): Safety instrumented systems, protective controls, and interlocks
- [Industrial Communications](industrial-communications/README.md): Fieldbus, industrial Ethernet, protocols, gateways, and integration
- [Industrial Information Systems](industrial-information/README.md): Historians, manufacturing execution systems, and process information
- [Operations & Engineering](operations-engineering/README.md): Commissioning, configuration, maintenance, and engineering workflows

## Organizational Principles

- **Industrial System Function** is the primary navigation dimension.
- **Industry Sector**, **System or Component Type**, **Architectural Model**, and **Technology or Vendor** remain distinct classification dimensions, not new root directories.
- Subjects may span multiple functions. Give each canonical subject one navigation home and represent other relationships with explicit links.
- Shared concepts belong in their canonical location; industrial implementations may refer to other knowledge domains and Foundations.
- Industrial Knowledge focuses on industrial systems and associated operational technology, while broader OT relationships may be expressed through the knowledge graph.
- The categories are a pragmatic navigation scheme, not a claim to reproduce a formal standards taxonomy.
- Deeper subject taxonomies remain undefined until approved.

## Unified Functional Navigation

This domain follows **Domain → Function → Implementation**. The approved categories above provide primary functional navigation; implementation-specific pages may be introduced under the relevant function when their canonical placement is established. Alternate perspectives should link to canonical pages rather than duplicate them.
