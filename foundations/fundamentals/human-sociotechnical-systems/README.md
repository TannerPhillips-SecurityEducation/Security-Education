## Development and review boundaries

**All 8/8 approved subjects passed the [48-family Wave B Batch 05 technical review](../WAVE-B-TECHNICAL-REVIEW-05.md).** The review corrected the distinction between unintentional human error and deliberate procedural violation. Each article remains **Core claims checked** as its source-review state; none is independently human peer-reviewed, fully source-verified or finally published.

These headings form a navigational area, not a newly authorized universal taxonomy of cognitive or ethical primitives. They maintain separate ownership of perception, decision, interaction, accessibility, error, collaboration, authority and responsibility, while linking to general evidence, data and systems principles. No assessments, grades or mandatory policies were introduced. G-01 mathematics, G-04 temporality and G-06 AI/ML now have canonical owners and scoped-reviewed articles; stronger source verification remains open.
