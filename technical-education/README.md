# Technical Education

**Status: Draft**

## Learning purposes

- **Knowledge Transfer:** Develop understanding of concepts and models.
- **Skill Development:** Develop the capacity to perform tasks.
- **Mindset Development:** Develop habits of interpretation, evaluation, and reasoning.

## Assessment roots

1. **Knowledge Checks:** Assess conceptual knowledge and understanding.
2. **Practical Assessments:** Assess application through tasks, exercises, and scenarios.

The learning purposes and assessment formats are distinct dimensions. Labs and lessons may address multiple learning purposes and assessment types.
